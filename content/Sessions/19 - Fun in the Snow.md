<h2><span class="hide-data" data-calendar="Faerun Calendar" data-date="1501-Nightal-29" data-end="" data-name="Fun in the Snow" data-category="Character Journal">A party for Lila - fun in the snow</span></h2>
As we all emerged from our storm shelter into the first hints of sun, it seemed like we would be able to relocate to the Singing Dryad Inn. Lila was barely able to sit still, knowing that her quiet complaint had been heard and that we were all going to draw together and do our very best to celebrate her birthday, and surviving the storm. Jerana stood at her side, still looking fragile, but present.

A careful patch of blankets stretched over the wide hole in the roof. Mara was able to cook up a plain broth, which was at least filling and better than nothing. There was little left to spice it and nothing for a sweet treat.

A few of the townspeople remained within the temple, recovering from the winter illness.

Berrus tried to organize our way forward bringing his thoughts into focus:
\- warmth (wood)
\- food (hunting)
\- sickness (healing)
\- morale

Hank looked for a way to liven things up. Fanciful minor illusions sparkled into existence and faded quickly. He invited the townspeople out into the snow into the magical field of Nathair's Mischief. It's hard to say if they were really entertained. They were certainly quite confused by the wild chaos.

Doran gathered the children and taking a ruined table, dragged them about on the snow to gales of laughter.

Berrus starts a snowball fight (with Elementalism / Beckon Water) with Phelan

Heswyn remained back in the temple, tending to those still sick so that they will not feel left behind in the celebrations. He told stories and waxed lyrical about the moon. But he did briefly shows up the party. His presence was a solid comfort.

Phelan starts up a happy birthday song, and Bracken parts with her little tart, gifting first one half ... and then the rest (minus a tiny bite).

Tyalië begins to carve a draft horse to go with Lila's doll. She works for a while and then quietly slips out, and on her way back to the Temple notices a figure just to the south of the Singing Dryad -- Garren has made his way to a quiet spot away from everyone else. She approaches him to speak -- and in connecting tries to comfort him in his guilt at surviving.

Tyalië passed Doran on the way to the Temple, and he noticed a figure over by the barracks. He called for Tyalië to stop, and the two of them attempted to figure out what is going on over there. Doran takes the children he is sledding back to the inn while Aiwë circles the barracks.

Doran mobilized those in the Inn and they tried to figure out who might not be present, but it was a difficult task. Phelan and Hank did their best to get a head-count, and tried to coordinate with Heswyn in the temple. Phelan found from Mara that some food had gone missing. Berrus joined us out by the Barracks as we investigated, finding a person's footprints, and better explored the wolf footprints around the northwest side of the building. We could not determine anything in particular, and so decided to bring some of the food from the stores back with us, or perhaps some firewood as an explanation of why we had been out for so long.

Unfortunately ... the barracks were occupied. Two Winter Wolves rose slowly to their feet and attacked
