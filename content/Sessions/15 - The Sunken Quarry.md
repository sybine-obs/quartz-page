# Searching the Sunken Quarry

## Diving in the lake

![[Images/Misc/Giant Venomous Snake.png#edge| circle right hsmall]]It is not my nature to put myself forward and lead the way, but as I looked out across the cold, dark waters and sunken buildings that filled the quarry, I knew that it was time to do so. I offered to explore as I was uniquely able, the abilities newly awoken from our time spent in the wilds. I called it to me and took the shape of a giant snake, able to wend its way easily through the waters and tight spaces in the deep.

We could sense in the depths a spark of something magic -- only one item, and it was something small and possibly innocuous. But it was the only thing that called out as we tried to determine where the anvil might be. As I approached that faint beacon, I felt a troubling in the water and called out in greeting to it. What was likely an elemental of the water itself seemed confused by my presence, and lashed out at my greeting. I retreated.

![[Images/Misc/Water Elemental.png| hsmall left lp]]Phelan encouraged Bracken to take the form of a small octopus and was able to sneak in and recover what turned out to be a potion of water breathing -- something that might be eminently useful in the future.

Still this was not the anvil, and the ruins were such that we could sift through them for days and still not find what we sought -- if it was even to be found in a usable state. As I spiralled out through the waters in my search, I encountered a passageway that dipped down, and a bit further arced back up and emerged in a cavern. A faint luminescence drew me forward and I was able to spit the light-charmed rock Heswyn provided further into the cave to get a better look. The passage climbed abruptly upwards, and opened into a much larger cavern. I retreated to inform the others of my finds and we decided this was our path forward.

## The crystal cavern

![[Images/Banners/Crystal Cave.png#edge| circle hsmall right lp]]We were able to jump from the quarry wall to the water below and dive through the passage without much issue (although I was forced to leave a grumpy Aiwë behind). The slimy muck defied our first attempts to ascend the passageway but with stubborn perseverance we were able to surmount that obstacle. Poor Doran took the worst of it, his size and solidity only making the slippery muck that much worse. Once there we encountered a strange elemental of rock. It spoke, but in no tongue we could understand. We lost our chance to negotiate and our attempt seemed to enrage it, and it attacked.

(Ironvein Guardian - [Galeb Duhr](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195002-galeb-duhr))

It was a difficult battle. The creature summoned animated rocks to its defense. And while we coordinated well, it felt for too long that none of our blows could land, or deeply damage the creatures. The cavern itself rose against us, rocks falling, and thunderous waves exploding out of the crystalline elements. Eventually we prevailed, battered but unbroken.
