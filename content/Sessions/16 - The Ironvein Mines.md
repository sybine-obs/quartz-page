# Searching for the anvil

## Patching up our wounds

![[Images/Battle maps/Ironvein Quarry.png#edge|cover circle wmedium right lp]]As the last echoes faded we took stock of our situation. Doran had weathered the worst of it and somehow came out looking not entirely battered, whereas Berrus had certain seen better days but refused help. And Heswyn -- well at least Heswyn was still with us, for there was a moment as the rock crashed down upon him that I thought we'd never see him again.
We cleaned our wounds and patched ourselves up as best we were able, poking around this cavern to reveal whatever secrets it might still hold. The crystals seemed interesting, so we gathered a few of those to see what Duran might make of them back at Snowmantle.
I took a moment to tell Doran how glad we were to have him with us now.

> "The mountain's roots run deep so it can weather the storm."

The others agreed, it was good to have him with us. In truth, it was good to have all of us.

After one last regretful moment for the loss of the elemental clearly guarding it's own domain, Heswyn was able to reassure us that it had merely been returned to its elemental plane and not permanently destroyed. Maybe it will find its way back, eventually.

## Climbing the steep passage

![[Images/Misc/Giant Wolf Spider.png#edge|cover circle hsmall wsmall left lp]]The way up to the east was steep enough to be considered a cliff, with a ledge part way up the path. The walls were a loose shale, promising to be difficult to scale. There was a moment where I heard what seemed to be impossible -- voices from above. I warned the others and Bracken went to take a look. Sure enough, another group was working to clear the caves of any valuable item.
To scale the face before us, I took on the form of a giant spider, and Phelan was able to ride my back up to the first ledge where we secured a rope to help others ascend. Despite that, the cliff still crumbled out from under the others as they climbed making far too much noise to be overlooked. And before we could ascend the second half of the obstacle, a figure greeted us from above, looking over the edge.

## Bargaining with the rival party

![[Images/NPCs/The opposing party.png#edge|cover circle right wmedium lp]]They looked to be a rough bunch, well outfitted and wearing no token or insignia we immediately recognized. Their leader questioned us, and questioned our presence at this very moment when they had been paid to be here and bring back all of value from the cavern. I questioned that as well. Quite a coincidence that in all the years and all the days that had passed between the anvil's loss and this moment, only now would two parties meet in the same moment to contest its return to the light of day.
Phelan undertook the negotiation and was able to bargain for a serviceable anvil and our peace while they took whatever else remained in the cavern. I had to trust his judgement in this, but a part of me worried on it. Was that the right choice? What if what we had bargained for was less than what we actually sought. The book we found implied that there was something _special_ about the anvil we wanted to find. And yet any anvil would make the Snowmantle forge functional again. It would be enough. We entertained ourselves and waited for them to finish.

Just when it seemed everything would go smoothly and our next challenge would be how to remove this large and solid object from the caverns, the ground began to shake and groan. The cavern was filled with the sound of crystal shattering. And then there were noises of running, fleeing something unknown. Our party was of one mind as we quickly scurried up the rest of the cliff to find what had happened above.

## The anvil and the Challenge

![[Images/Misc/Anvil.png#edge|cover circle left wsmall lp]]Phelan and Bracken tracked the other group to see how far they had fled. They were arguing at the mouth of the tunnel out of the caverns. We began to explore as quickly and quietly as we were able.
The Anvil, and truely this was what we came to find, was easily visible having half-fallen into a pool at the center where a side passage opened into another small cavern. There was a feeling of portent as soon as one entered that cavern, an oppressive weight of something _watching_.
Heswyn opened himself to Selune and to whatever was trying to communicate with us and felt the nature of the challenge -- creation or destruction, it asked. Which did we pursue?
Creation, he answered, and in response the cavern caused the crystals to crawl and climb into a tall plinth next to the anvil. The challenge was set. Place the anvil on the plinth without shattering the crystal. He set about trying to figure out how to make that happen.
As Heswyn explained what he had learned, Berrus went in and started to demostrate "creating" by making a net. He encouraged me to join with him and I doubtfully sat down beside and took out my carving tools. Others tried to find tasks that might prove our intent, but Heswyn was finally able to ascertain and convey that it was a specific task we must all undertake together, that we must lift this anvil _together_ onto the fragile crystaline plinth without damaging it. This must be the task that the other group failed earlier, and the noises we heard suggested a violently negative outcome for failure.

It was no easy task, but with care and caution we were able to leverage all of our strengths to see it safely done. Doran's strength, my vision, Berrus's dexterity, and Heswyn and Phelan's support and guideance all came together to complete the task succesfully. The plinth shrunk into a wide crystal platform. In that moment, an elemental greater by far than the one we defeated in the lower caverns emerged from the wall and bowed to us. The Anvil was ours.

Only then did Phelan reveal that the other party was returning. Berrus thought quickly to deceive them, racing out with dire warnings of death and destruction. We all watched as the other group took this in, ready to disbelieve. But before they could advance further, the great elemental pushed out from the rock once more at Berrus's back. And the others fled.

## Recovering the Anvil

![[Images/Misc/Draft Horse.png#edge|cover right hsmall lp]]Suddenly we were confronted with a new challenge. This anvil was far greater and heavier than any of the others we had bargained to take earlier. Much more than we could easily carry. The way out of the cavern emerged from a cliff wall, high above unknown forest below, and with only a dubious scaffolding to allow the descent. The other passage went out only to the lake.

But we had found the potion of water-breathing earlier! We debated the best way to use it. Could Doran carry the anvil out? Despite his great size and strength he swore he could not. But maybe something large and strong could drag it... And so I drew upon the forms of nature once more and, after taking the potion myself, shaped into a draft horse. The others tied the anvil to me and I was able to drag it out across the lake bottom. Thankfully, the rest swam along with, diving down to assist as they were able. We emerged on the other side of the lake before the potion ran out. <span data-calendar="Faerun Calendar" data-date="1501-Uktar-13" data-end="1501-Uktar-15" data-name="The Trek back to Snowmantle" data-category="Character Journal"></span>

## Return to Snowmantle

From there it was a matter of retracing our steps. We walked back the way we came, dragging the anvil in turns. It was a grueling journey, but worth it in the end for the amazement of our people as we returned to town.

Duran is sure he will be able to do great things with this anvil in his forge.
