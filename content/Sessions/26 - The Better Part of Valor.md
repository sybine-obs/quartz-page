# Fleeing the Ogre Cave

## Facing off on Two Fronts

<span style="font-style:italic;font-weight:bold" class="hide-data" data-calendar="Faerun Calendar" data-date="1502-Hammer-20" data-end="" data-name="Over land and the river under " data-category="Character Journal">Hammer 20 (evening/night) - Fleeing over (and under) mountain</span>

Berrus, Doran, Hank, and Heswyn faced off against the ogres that came out of the cave, leading them on a slow battle of sorts, but mostly dodging and retreating to draw them away. It could have gone much worse, and thankfully they were able to fully retreat once Bracken came with news of our (hopefully) imminent escape.

![[Images/Battle maps/OgreRavineBattle.png#edge|cover wmedium center lp]]

Phelan and I took different routes to end up in the same place -- to flee through the waterway and pray it was a safe exit from the cavern. The ogres and at least one Hill Giant were hard on our heels, even as I tried to slow them with the entangling, blooming flowers that my grandmother's circlet could conjure. I traded a look with Phelan as I ran past and I think we both understood. This was a huge risk, but one that seemed necessary to take. As we all crowded in to the small cavern, Phelan cast out a magic that settled over us all leaving us with clear minds in this fraught moment, and sheltering us from fear as we leapt into the waters below.

## Swimming out of the cave

As those outside drew further away from the ogres and finally were able to successfully able to deter further pursuit, Phelan and I struggled to lead the Goliaths through the underground waterway. It was dark, and frigid. The way was twisted, but water continued to flow outward, giving me hope we would eventually find the light of day once again. Phelan held his spell which might have been the one thing that allowed our ultimate success.

We emerged through the frozen ceiling of water that capped the river as it emerged from the cave system. The Goliaths smashed their weigh upwards and we crawled out. Sava was one of the last and she dragged the limp body of Phelan after her out onto solid ground. I rushed over to see to him, casting out for healing with a sharp _wyrd_. I had almost forgotten the stars that still covered me, and basked for just a moment in the warmth that washed through me as well with that spell.

## Trek back to camp

<span style="font-style:italic;font-weight:bold" class="hide-data" data-calendar="Faerun Calendar" data-date="1502-Hammer-21" data-end="" data-name="The trek back to camp" data-category="Character Journal">Hammer 21 - The morning trek back to camp</span>

Phelan woke, and we gathered close with the Goliaths to determine our next steps. I wrung water from our clothes as fast as I was able, but we were all dull and shaking with exhaustion now. We began the slow trudge back to the mesa's summit where hopefully the rest of our group would be waiting.

## Goliath reunion

The look on Doran's face as we emerged, climbing up the to the mesa summit, was that of deep relief and yet we could not miss the pain as well at the small numbers we had managed to extract from the cave complex. **Of those we broke out from the cages, only 28 had made it back to camp with us**, lost to attacks or the cold waters on the way out, I could not say.

Now the next questions began to press on me. We had been gone much longer than planned from Snowmantle, and we would be returning with even more mouths to feed. How will we continue to see to their safety, and what will we find when we get back?
