# Searching for more direction

## Headed towards the trail and trail-end

<span style="font-style:italic;font-weight:bold" class="hide-data" data-calendar="Faerun Calendar" data-date="1502-Hammer-19" data-end="" data-name="Finding the Ogre lair..." data-category="Character Journal">Hammer 19 - Thick mist obscures the way as we head down into the valley</span>
I had hoped that in the morning, the way forward might be revealed under the early sun, but thick mist had rolled in and obscured everything. We discussed what I had seen from the ridge and debated leaving information at the standing stone marker, or investigating the trail and cleft that might shelter a cave. We started out, quickly finding that climbing downhill is hard work on the ankles, and descended from the top of the butte into the ravine below.
We crossed a solidly frozen river, and more bare rock, both dusted with patchy snow. The frozen river provided a clear path forward. There were no obvious tracks in this area. Looking up the path we could see the walls close until they met in a very narrow cleft.

Here there were fresh tracks cross the ravine, heavy and too big for wolves or other beasts. While there were obviously a few smaller booted prints, they were still larger than Doran's feet. Up high along the ravine was an odd pile of boulders and rocks, and as we pondered that, we also came across a "nest" of branches. Hidden there was a small stone on a thong, carved with the symbol of Doran's people, a shallow carving of two peaks, side by side.

Deep inside the cleft, Berrus heard a low, intermittent sound in a guttural language.

We did everything we could to figure out what was ahead. Bracken flitted in and returned, thinking she saw two ogres. We pooled our knowledge of ogres and I suddenly remembered one tome I was forced to study, filled with vibrant art of shockingly gory scenes... _(History dirty 20)_

> [!info|color-red background-yellow] Ogres and Hill Giants
>
> - _Ogres will frequently ally with or are dominated by Hill Giants. In the Northern Hills they're known to keep rough livestock pens and use simple ambushes rather than open tactics. They speak Giant and a broken form of common. Their hides are tough. They have little discipline once a fight turns against them._
> - _In the Dagger Hills and the edges of the thunder peaks. Ogre bands have grown more common in recent years since larger threats. Push them into contested ground. They rarely hold territory for long (unless they're large or they're being controlled or manipulated by something more powerful). Captives taken by ogres are usually kept for labor or food. Escape is possible if the ogres are distracted or stronger force intervenes._
> - _Some ogre groups in these hills have learned to work with prepared rockfalls and to post lazy but effective watches at caves. When a hill giant would take control of them, they become more dangerous because the giant enforces crude order and keeps them from scattering at the first sign of resistance._

We debated setting the trap on what was in the cave, and other options, but ended up with the understanding we needed more information, and Bracken wouldn't be able to get it for us. We triggered the rockfall to see what would emerge.

Two ogres came to investigate the rockfall. I attempted to have Aiwë lure them off, but unfortunately they took her appearance as a deer to attack with a rock and she was dispersed back into spirit form. Hank tried to confuse them with an illusion, and that also failed to draw out more from the cave. We began to sneak around to see what else we could learn, and the worst happened _(nat1 stealth)_. I tripped and stumbled into plain view of both of the Ogres. Actin on an instinct that perhaps I could draw them off, I loudly taunted them both and then took the form of a snow martin and ran as fast as I was able...

## Run, run, run!

We fled as fast as we were able, and thankfully the Ogres did not further attack and only gave half-hearted chase. We regrouped on the slope where we had camped the night prior, rejoining Durin and Garran there. It was in that moment that Berrus was able to relate what he had seen -- the hand of a Goliath strung on sinew around one of the ogres' necks, as a macabre decoration.

It was too much for Dorin, and we could all see the rage decend upon him as he stormed out into the snow. I couldn't leave him alone with that, and followed. It was some time before he had the sense enough to return and we bedded down for the night with the resolution of finding another way into the caves on the following day.

## Another way in to the cave

![[Images/Misc/OgreCave.png#edge|cover circle right hsmall lp]]<span style="font-style:italic;font-weight:bold" class="hide-data" data-calendar="Faerun Calendar" data-date="1502-Hammer-20" data-end="" data-name="Searching for another way in" data-category="Character Journal">Hammer 20 - Searching for another way</span>
The morning arrived with sharper winds and a peppering of snow mixed with sleet.  We knew the Ogres had Doran's people. We suspected that they had at least one Hill Giant among them, for otherwise they would not have been so circumspect and organized. A frontal assault (without a great amount of outside help) would be certain death for us all.

I thought perhaps we could bait them out, or trade enough food that the Goliaths would  no longer be the immediate choice, but I could not locate the herd of snow elk I knew to be in the area, perhaps we could make a trade. Unfortunately, they were not to be found.

We thought maybe to split the party and draw out the Ogres and then slip inside to see what could be found or made to happen... but we went round and round with those ideas and came up with nothing that seemed it could be work. Instead, we hiked the ridge of the ravine and around the side of the mountain, searching for another way in.

We found only one passageway that might give us entrance, and it was too small for all of us to go down. Phelan would fit. We joined our ropes together and helped him climb down inside. What he found there was formidable -- a hill giant working over a cookfire, and the sounds of more ogres than we had hoped. He climbed back up even as we searched for another option. There was nothing to be found. We opted to wait for darkness, hoping to infiltrate the caves and learn more as the inhabitants slept.

This time I descended with Phelan, slipping through passages that would have been too small by slithering down them as a snake. We dropped into the caverns and began to search.

The cooking area was a terrible sight, for all around one side were great cages holding what was left of Doran's clan. We urged them to be patient and wait, as we tried to explore further. We climbed to a smaller passage above the cooking area, too small to fit an ogre or giant, but perhaps the Goliaths could sneak out this way. We found a possible way out -- water down below in a passage or another cavern? Too dark to tell. Further along, we saw the back of an Ogre clearly set to guard another small cave. And within ... three Goliath children.

The Goliath clan would never leave without them ...
