# The Temple of Cyric

## What remains in the High Priest's Quarters

We ended our trek through the temple back above ground in what must have been taken over as the high priest's quarters, whatever it may have been before that. The room was relatively well preserved, all things considered. Lanseril's thorns had kept out much of the elements that would have reduced all within to rot and dust.

Still, there was not much to be salvaged. We did find a heavy iron lockbox, and while my skills did not suffice, Berrus was able to teas the lock open.

> - a good scattering of gold and gems (300gp worth, including one 100gp pearl which will work for Identify spell)
> - fine cloak (cloak of protection +1)
> - ring of quality make (ring of mind shielding)
> - ritual dagger (not of Cyric) - silvered (counts as a magical dagger for resistance)

(Also identified, the ring Berrus foud earlier: Ring of Protection +1)

The heavy desk was beautifully made, but old and weathered. Possibly this could be restored to some use, or the oak repurposed. It may have been taken from the Great Oak that was felled at the northwest of town, but it seems a shame to simply scrap and burn it.

As we emerged, the townspeople clamored to know what had passed. Phelan was kind enough to step forward and field their many questions, spinning our efforts into quite the exciting tale.  (Donated the 10 recovered/purified rations)

Jerana wanted to see what remained, and so we saw her through the temple once more, revealing what had been destroyed to cleanse it. She agreed that any who come in to see it cleaned out or restored should be of strong mind and stout spirit.

## Called to say Goodbye to Ellowyn

As we emerged once again from the temple, the day now well spent, Bracken and Aiwë indicated something to the northwest. There was a call. As we crested the hill there, Ellowyn revealed herself once more, no longer as bitterly angry as she had been on our first meeting.

> I will leave you with a gift -- the things you have brought with you, I will awaken them

(personal specialty items are now active and attuned)

At the last, she bade us plant the acorn used to find the axe to the north, that something new might come of it.

## Exploring to the Northeast

![[Images/Travel Maps/NE of Snowmantle.png#edge|cover circle hsmall left lp]]
We left the town to track where the Ironvein Quarry might have been, with little more than the hints of the book to guide us. Luckily, the path to such a place must have been worn deep with the weight of what had been borne upon it and its memory was still present in the lay of the land.
On our way there, we came a cross an old shrine to Mielikki. Berrus spotted a box of healing herbs, and there were more growing thickly about to be gathered for later use. Perhaps Wren can brew them into a beneficial potion. And somehow surviving for who knows how long -- a well made winter cloak.

Further to the northwest, a collapsed cabin appeared on the horizon. The trail was marked with the print of a wolf far larger than any natural beast. A Dire Wolf, no doubt. And that guess was proved a certainty as we moved to camp against the side of the cabin and use its wall for shelter. Three dire wolves emerged, growling as we approached what they had claimed as a den. It was luck that let us divert from there without picking a fight with the creatures. (Successful Animal Handling check)

Far enough away from the cabin to feel safe, we found a clearing to set up camp and planned night watches.

> 1st: Berrus, Heswyn
> 2nd: Brendor, Phelan
> 3rd: Doran, Tyalië

![[Images/Misc/Displacer Beast.png|cover circle hsmall right lp]]Hardly into our first watch, Aiwë sensed something approaching, but we could not see what it was. I went to alert Berrus, but by then it was too late. A dark, cat-like creature sprang out of the woods and attacked!
It seemed almost impossibly hard to hit, but the light of the feywilds clung to it as I called upon their fires, and the illumination seemed to draw it into a more cohesive shape. We were able to defeat it, and with some effort claim its hide, and a writhing tentacle, possibly to fashion something useful later. Brendor recognized it as a displacer beast -- something I hope not to have to face again soon.
