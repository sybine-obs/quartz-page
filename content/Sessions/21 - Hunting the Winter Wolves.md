# The direction of the hunt

## Hunting the Wolves

<span style="font-style:italic;font-weight:bold" class="hide-data" data-calendar="Faerun Calendar" data-date="1502-Hammer-8" data-end="" data-name="Hunting the wolves" data-category="Character Journal">Hammer 8</span>
As the day dawned clear enough for us to think we could leave the town safely for a short while, we debated the best direction to take. The Grove and the Quarry might hold enough game to support us, but were also a further trek. The present danger of the winter wolves to our west seemed more pressing. While wolf meat would not be the most savory, it was still food. And leaving them at our backs to harry the camp while we were gone was too great of a risk. We decided to hunt them instead of leaving them to harry our people.

We began to track the wolves...![[Images/Misc/Tracks in the snowy woods.png#edge|lp hsmall right circle ]]

- they went this way to the south and west
- at least one more larger wolf joined them before the woods
- the booted prints continue after the wolves and they continue to follow the wolf prints

As we walked along the track, the forest rose up to one side and then slowly enveloped us. Spruce trees stretched out their fragrant boughs, blocking sight into the deeper forest. At one point the tracks swung wide around a deep "crib", and we started to circle it as well. But seeing old structures that used to bridge the gap, Hank climbed down into it to explore. I was surprised, but there were a few old things to be found in the defile.

- Old metal rails for a sled --
- Tarred box - two tiny unhappy spiders, coil of "good" rope, rusty hook, small tin (firemaking kit), 3 dry torches
- Giant Snow Marten chittering in the tree -- this is a NEW FORM for Tyalië

We found a fallen tree to build a camp against, butting our shelter up against the torn roots and within the sheltered depression where it was torn from the ground.
We slept well enough and woke up warm and rested.

## Hunting the Wolves - Day 2

<span  style="font-style:italic;font-weight:bold" class="hide-data" data-calendar="Faerun Calendar" data-date="1502-Hammer-9" data-end="" data-name="Hunting the wolves - day 2" data-category="Character Journal">Hammer 9</span>
The paths were deeper here, and we spotted a few drops of blood from time to time along the trail.
The trunks on the left carried old blazes, grown shut with time. But under the old blazes, there were new ones - two strokes cut into the bark and fresh enough that they are still sticky with sap.

Eventually we came to a place where the trail fell into a deep, dark notch and all around the spruce trees were broken in a huge fan, as if something had crashed through them long ago. On the wind that blew from the deep cut came the scent of wet fur and the faintest hint of cured leather, like the hide of a drum cured by a fire.

Scouting, Aiwë reported back of the trail ahead, the broken trees, and a little open area beyond within the depression. Bracken saw more in the area past the broken trees. The open area just within the cut was filled with packed snow and ice, and to one side a winter wolf watched the trail we currently followed. He seemed to detect Bracken somehow, perhaps by scent. In the darkness she saw two more shapes moving. Three wolves.
Circling further, Aiwë spotted a glint of light on the ridge above the cut and heard the click of something metal. Combined with that information, and the blood dotting the trail, recent blazes, and even the figure seen in our home camp, I wondered if there wasn't a hunter that might be added to our number in the effort to bring down the wolves. Phelan and I shared our information and Doran, Berrus and Phelan decided to head to the ridge and see if there was another enemy, or a possible ally there.

On the ridge, they encountered Kresh Two-Tusk, an orc scout of the tribes that had driven Doran from his people to look for less contested lands. Doran was not happy to find the orc here and was understandably hostile at first, but the three managed to come to an agreement with Kresh and his other scouts. They had a young orc held by the wolves, likely to be their next meal. We could take the spoils of the battle if they could recover their youngling. They would try to draw one or more of the wolves away to divide the battleground. <span style="display: flex; justify-content: center;">![[Images/NPCs/Kresh Two-Tusk.png#edge|lp cover ]]</span>

They agreed, and we prepared to assault the den, taking care to spread out that we would not be caught together in the devastating cold breath of the wolves. As we came within range, Kresh's drums rang out, and the wolves flinched with every beat.

<span style="display: flex; justify-content: center;">![[Images/Misc/Fighting Winter Wolves.png#edge|lp  cover wmedium]]</span>

The melee was intense. Hank flung illusions in every direction, adding to the chaos of battle and confusing the wolves. They eventually focused on him and brought him down, although Heswyn was quick with a healing word in Hank's defense. Doran distracted one, and we were able to focus on another. It fell first to Berrus' swift strikes. The second dropped soon after, and the third fell to the scout's team.
The last wolf, unseen until later in the battle, looked around at the field of its fallen pack members, and fled. <span style="display: flex; justify-content: center;">![[Images/Battle maps/Winter Wolf Lair.png#edge|lp cover  wmedium]]</span>
