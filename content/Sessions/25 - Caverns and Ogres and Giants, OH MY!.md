# Rescuing the Two Crags Clan

## Getting the Goliaths out of the cavern

<span style="font-style:italic;font-weight:bold" class="hide-data" data-calendar="Faerun Calendar" data-date="1502-Hammer-20" data-end="" data-name="Caverns and Ogres and Giants, OH MY!" data-category="Character Journal">Hammer 20 (evening/night) - Having infiltrated the ogre's cave, we're about to make something happen</span>
Tyalië and Phelan returned to the fire and food-prep cavern, casting Pass Without a Trace and Guidance on the way. The Hill Giant was still sleeping.
Tyalië managed to lockpick three of five cages, freeing approximately 21 (of 35) Goliaths. Under the umbrella of Pass Without a Trace, these Goliaths climbed up and hid in the rocky passage while Phelan and Tyalië determined what to do next. Of those Goliaths, one (Sava Stonebind) hesitated and directly followed Tyalië between cages. She convinced the others to wait, especially once hearing of the children and Goliath equipment held in a nearby cave

> [!important|background-yellow color-red] Sava Stonebind
> _Doran’s childhood friend. Goliath, late twenties, hunter. ![[Images/NPCs/SavaStonebind.png#edge|cover right circle  wtiny lp]]  She was the one who stayed when he went south to learn how the lowland folk kept from starving. She led the decision to move. Not warm about it. She will not pretend the ridge was still livable, and she will not thank him for being right. She is the best tracker in the group and the one the others look at when a choice has to be made tonight. She still wears the notch he cut into her spear-haft the winter they both took their first elk._

Phelan used Bracken as best he could to coordinate with the group outside the cavern. Another of the Goliaths (Brask) returned to the cavern with the cages, preparing to try and get the last two open. Doran, Heswyn, Hank and Berrus returned to the cave mouth, planning to make a ruckus to draw out the ogres and make it easier to rescue the rest of the Goliaths. Tyalië staged herself to rush the lone guard on the children.

> [!important|background-yellow color-red] Brask
> _Young. Sixteen. Sava’s nephew, or close enough that the distinction does not matter. ![[Images/NPCs/Brask.png#edge|cover right circle  wtiny lp]] Fast, proud, and angry that the first thing he saw in the south was a logging town living inside a dead temple. He is a good shot and a bad listener. He will attach himself to Doran or he will attach himself to the first fight that looks like it matters. Either way he is going to be a problem until he is given a real task._

Eventually, the planning became action. Hank was spotted on his way to cast illusions in front of the guards at the cave entrance. All four outside the cave mouth sprang into action, dodging and evading as much as possible while still attempting to draw more enemies out of the cave.

> [!abstract|background-yellow color-red] Various effects of Hank's "Nathir's Mischief" spell
>
> 1. A brief, realistic illusion of a shifting shadow or closing door appears in a space you can see within 60 feet. One creature that sees it must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom save or hesitate, losing its reaction this round.
> 2. The air fills with the sudden smell of blood or rotting meat for 1 minute in a 10-foot radius. Creatures in the area have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.
> 3. Drops of molasses appear and hover in the cube, turning it into difficult terrain until the start of your next turn.

Inside, Tyalië led the bulk of the Goliaths to attack the guard and free the children. Unfortunately, they were spotted and more Ogres and a Hill Giant converged on the tunnel junction. They felled one Ogre, and Tyalië ensnared the others that approached within the entangling grasp of the Moonflower vines from her grandmother's circlet. The Hill Giant took the opportunity to throw a stone, partially crushing Tyalië, but not dropping her. By some bit of luck, her concentration on the magic of the vines held, and she was able to dodge the incoming attacks of the two nearby ogres. She held her ground there, poised to run to the east and the dubious exit of the underwater passage.

Meanwhile Phelan looked for the cage key, and Brask picked up a cleaver to use as a weapon. When the distraction began outside the cave, Brask broke one cage open with a mighty blow _(nat20)_ and Phelan broke open the second cage with an eldritch blast. The noise and commotion both in the cavern and outside the cave entrance woke the Hill Giant. She sounded the alarm, but was confused by Phelan's Calm Emotions spell, falling quiet and lethargic under its soothing effect.
