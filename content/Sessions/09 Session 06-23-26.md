The men say there are two of them. Sisters, they call them. I do not know if that is truth or superstition. What I do know is that the packs do not hunt without their leave. That alone makes them more dangerous than any warlord we have dealt with in these hills._
>
> _**Entry 3 – Intercepted Message (Dated 3 Tarsakh)** The Sisters grow bolder. Three nights ago they allowed their packs to strike one of our supply caravans moving through the northern pass. I have advised the cell in Dagger Falls to cease operations north of the old logging road until further notice. We are not yet ready to contest them directly. Whatever power they draw upon is older than ours, and far less predictable._
>
> _**Entry 4 – Final Entry (Dated 19 Mirtul)** We attempted contact again. This time, one of them answered. She did not speak. I do not believe they are interested in alliance. I do not believe they are interested in anything we have to offer. They are waiting for something. Or someone. And I do not think we want to be here when it arrives._
