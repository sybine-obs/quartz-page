# Absent for this session

## Willowbrook is under attack!
<span data-calendar="Faerun Calendar" data-date="1501-Mar-21" data-name="Road to Willowbrook" data-category="Character Journal"></span>


> [!abstract|background-yellow color-red] DM RECAP
> - As the caravan traveled toward Dagger Falls, the party spotted a large column of smoke rising on the northern horizon during the midnight watch. **Jerana Fisher** identified it as coming from Willowbrook, a small hamlet the Morn Riders often used as a staging point. Rather than risk the entire caravan, the group decided to leave the wagons in a safe location while a smaller party moved ahead to investigate. **Phelan** and **Tyalië** remained behind to help guard the settlers.
> - On the road north, the scouting party encountered **a lone Goliath named Doran**. He had also seen the smoke and was heading toward Willowbrook to investigate. After a brief conversation, **Doran joined the group**, and together they continued toward the village.
> - When they arrived, **they found Willowbrook under attack**. Three buildings were burning, including the central inn, and a gang of brigands was moving through the hamlet, looting and killing survivors. The party immediately moved to intervene.
> - The fight was intense. The bandits used a combination of bows and melee weapons. **Heswyn** attempted to reach several wounded villagers lying on the ground, but was struck multiple times by arrows from the bandit archers. Though grievously wounded, he managed to drag one villager to safety before collapsing. **Doran** charged into the melee with heavy swings, taking several solid hits but continuing to press forward. **Brendor** took up a crossbow and made effective use of it, picking off bandits and disrupting their numbers from range. **Berrus** moved with care, using cover and tactical positioning to engage key targets while avoiding unnecessary exposure.
> - **The bandit leader proved to be a tough and capable fighter**, but after a hard-fought battle, the party finally brought him down.
> - In the aftermath, **Heswyn** — despite his injuries — saw to the wounded and offered prayers for the dead. **Doran** dug graves for those who had fallen. Together with **Wren**, he also examined the bodies to ensure none were werewolves in disguise. At first, all the corpses had appeared human, but later examination confirmed that at least one was a werewolf. The surviving villagers were questioned and consoled. It became clear that **the Morn Riders had been caught largely inside the inn when it was set ablaze**.
> - With the immediate threat dealt with, the scouting party returned to the main caravan to bring the rest of the settlers to Willowbrook. The session ended as the party made its way back to escort the caravan to the village.
