## Mid-fight with the wolves

We extracted our party from the building (Doran and Tyalië looking quite a bit worse for wear) and as we did so, Heswyn arrived following Hank's little Tinker. His healing and the divine light of his aura came none too soon and were greatly welcomed. We rallied to face the wolves but our united front, and Hank's quick-cast bonfire, caused them to turn and slink away. An intelligent foe. It would have been better if we could have settled it then.

We did our best to clean up and head back to the Dryad in groups. There would be no food to return to Mara, and now we needed to not upset the settlers before the evening of celebration could come to a close. Morale was fragile. We did our best to not shatter it.

All the distractions of the evening had kept me from my original thought as I left the fireside in the Dryad. I hurried forward to catch Heswyn and check in with him, how are the people doing, how is he doing. I finally felt strong enough to offer to restore some of the people that are still sick. He accepted that help and also suggested talking to Wren about dealing with the Displacer Beast hide. I thought it might be difficult finding other elements this time of year, but it wouldn't hurt to know more. I sent Heswyn to check in with Garren, for I didn't think I left Garren in a good place and I'm of no use to soothe tender feelings.

Hank returned to the inn and the celebration, making a "music box" of sorts and gets Jerana to sing an old song of Teshendale.
Berrus picked up as he often does, playing with the kids. He kept his eyes open and ears tuned to any detail that might reveal who was communicating from within the town to the ridge at night.

![[Images/Misc/Rillifane Rallathil.png#edge|cover circle left hsmall lp]]I finally made it down into the temple and set myself to meditate and pray amongst the dirt and roots that had crumbled one of the exterior walls. I reached out to Rillithane and felt the understanding of how to restore a person to health filter into my mind, but something nagged me. **There might be another god that could lend us support in this deep cold, but who?** _(failed an investigation check (there may be some god in the Seldarine that has something to do with cold)_
After soothing those I could I went to find Wren. I was uncertain how to approach him, and apparently that didn't sit well with him (I suppose next time I'll just get right to the point.) In the end he shared that we will need about 2000gp and time to make the displacer beast cloak. Hank can help harvest the necessary ingredients.

**It was quite clear to us with the events of the evening. We will need to start rationing food after the party.**

As the party wound down, we gathered together to discuss how to ensure the further survival of the settlers. We opted to try to get the fishing weir in working order again, and set what snares we could for the next few days as we worked on snowshoes to extend our range for hunting. _(Crit on Survival)_ I was able to take a few hides and broken bits to get the southwest structure into something that would shelter us from a normal storm. From here we could watch the weir for tampering and use it as a base. _(Consumes all extra hides from party inventory.)_

Berrus dedicated himself to repairing and making more nets for the weir.

Doran and Hank had shocking success creating snowshoes for the entire party.
