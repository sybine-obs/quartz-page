# The long trek south and west
We tracked the two wolves that had taken Lila successfully for many hours, and night fell as we entered the abandoned mining town. 
The attack came as we searched the first building, before we could even move on to the rest of the settlement. They set fire to the structure to flush us out--successfully.

And when we finally confronted the pack, their leaders, two werewolves, made themselves known. One confronted us on the ground, while the other threatened to drop Lila from the cliffs above.

Boss Werewolf caught me approaching to possibly catch Lila, and picked me up by the throat -- whispered in my ear of a chance to save Lila in White Crag, wherever that might be.

# Trapped in the mine
We were forced to retreat into the open mouth of the abandoned mine, and the beast brought the front of it down around us, trapping us inside.
