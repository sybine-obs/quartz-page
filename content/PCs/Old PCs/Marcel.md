## Impressions:

- what's under the linen wrap on his shield?

Introduced in Session 04
NO LONGER PLAYING after Session 05

Marcel grew up the eldest son of fisher folk, spending long days on water ways pole or net fishing with his family. During a local competition of strength, he wrestled his long time friend Alesandra to a draw, splitting the prize and drawing the attention of a passing Purple Dragon Knight. She introduced herself to them afterwards and offered a spot in a upcoming tryout for squires. Both readily accepted and with encouragement from their families set out with Purple Dragon Knight Janine Gardet.

Both performed well and were soon accepted into squire training, over the course of several years, learning fighting, horsemanship, manners, and the lore and history of Corymr and the Purple Dragon Knights.

Now grown up, Marcel and his best friend soon found themselves facing one another during their final squires tourney. Having already exchanged two lance strikes each. With everything on the line for this final tilt, they charged head long into one another. Marcel for the briefest of moments looked to the crowd to see the crowd's reaction, thus pulling the tip of his lance up by an inch or two. When they hit, both lances struck true, but Marcel's rode high off the jousting shield and splintered into his friends fully helmed face. A splinter struck just right, flying through the visors eye slot into his friends eye. Knight Janine later said she had to of passed instantly, but this was little comfort to Marcel, whom had killed his best friend. The Knights were comforting and many said these things happen upon the training and battlefield alike. Marcel knew though, that it was pride that caused his lapse, and vowed never again.

Purple Dragon Knight Janine told him not flee from this, learn from it, grow from it, become a knight as bright and brave as his friend would have wanted. She said, "in this line of work, you may see many a companion fall to split second decisions. Strive to do better, study harder, plan better, and perhaps you can insure more make it home, then not."
