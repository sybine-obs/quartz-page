> [!important|color-red background-yellow lp] Old Thom Kettlefish
> ![[Images/NPCs/Caravan/Old Thom Kettlefish.png#edge|cover right circle  wtiny lp]]
> An elderly Teshendale descendant, Old Thom is a wiry, weathered man in his 60s with a crooked back, threadbare cloak, and a perpetual pipe clenched in his teeth. His face is lined like old leather, eyes twinkling with mischief even in the worst storms. He carries a battered satchel of stories and a voice like gravel wrapped in warmth.
>
> He sits on the back of a wagon, legs dangling, gesturing with gnarled hands as he spins tales of the old Tesh — fish that jumped into nets, drowned lovers, and the river that never forgets. His laugh is a raspy cackle that cuts through the wind, and his stories keep the settlers going when hope runs thin.

- Spent time with Phelan, who learns not only does Old Thom have a bunch of stories, but also particularly loves sweets. Or seems to, since much of what he acquires ends up back in the hands of the many children of the caravan
