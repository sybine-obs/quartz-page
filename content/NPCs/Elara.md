> [!important|color-red background-yellow lp] Elara Fisher
> ![[Images/NPCs/Caravan/Elara Fisher.png#edge|cover right circle htiny wtiny lp]]
> Jerana’s granddaughter and the caravan’s young scout, Elara is a lithe, freckled human woman of 19 with wild auburn hair tied back in a ponytail. She wears practical long-sleeved leather armor and a simple tunic, dagger always at her hip. Energetic and quick-witted, she whistles tunes while scouting and has a fearless grin that hides a sharp eye for danger.
>
> She’s the first to volunteer for ridge watches, races across hills for fun, and dreams aloud of fishing the restored Tesh like her great-grandfather.
