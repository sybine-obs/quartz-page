#NPC

> [!important|background-yellow icon-red] Lila Waters
> ![[Images/NPCs/Caravan/Lila Waters.png#edge||cover right circle htiny wtiny lp]]
> Jerana Fisher's great-niece and the caravan's youngest orphan, Lila is a wide-eyed halfling girl of 10, standing barely 3'0" tall. She has dark curly pigtails tied with scraps of cloth, a smattering of freckles across her nose, and huge curious brown eyes that miss nothing. She wears a patched, oversized dress that once belonged to her mother, and she never goes anywhere without clutching her rag doll—stuffed with straw, one button eye missing, arms threadbare from endless hugging.
>
> Lila is shy around strangers but clings to the older adventurers like a little sister, tugging sleeves and whispering questions: "Is the ghost real? Can I hold your sword?" Her voice is small and sweet, but when she's scared she hides behind Jerana's cloak and peeks out with those big eyes.
