> [!important|color-red background-yellow lp] Mara Greenhand
> ![[Images/NPCs/Caravan/Mara Greenhand.png#edge|cover right circle htiny wtiny lp]]A sturdy, no-nonsense human woman of 35, Mara stands 5'7" with brown skin weathered by years in the fields, strong callused hands, and a practical green headscarf tied back to keep sweat and flour out of her eyes. She carries a worn herbal satchel at her hip, always ready with pouches of dried thyme, feverfew, and whatever roots she’s foraged.

Her face is set in a skeptical frown most days, lines already forming from squinting at the sun and at dreamers who talk more than they work. She kneads dough with rhythmic, forceful strokes while giving blunt advice: “Dreams’re fine, but bellies need fillin’.”

>

Practical to a fault, she’s the caravan’s voice of reason—grounded, resourceful, and quick to point out when plans are too pretty and not enough bread.
