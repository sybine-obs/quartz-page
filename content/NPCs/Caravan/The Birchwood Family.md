#NPC

> [!important|background-yellow icon-red] The Birchwood Family
> ![[Images/NPCs/Caravan/Birchwood Family.png#edge||cover right circle htiny wtiny lp]]
>
> - **Tomas Birchwood** (24, human male) – Cobbler **Personality Traits:** Practical, steady, and quietly optimistic. He has a dry sense of humor and is generous with his time and skills, often repairing boots for other settlers late into the night. He is level-headed in a crisis but can become withdrawn when worried about the future. **Background:** His ancestors fled Teshendale during its fall. Tomas carries a small heirloom awl that once belonged to his great-grandfather, and he hopes to one day open a proper cobbler’s shop in the new Dale.
> - **Elara Birchwood** (23, human female) – Weaver **Personality Traits:** Warm, sociable, and naturally optimistic. She is a gifted storyteller who uses her weaving to create small gifts (scarves, patches, blankets) that lift the spirits of other settlers. She is slightly anxious about the dangers of the road but hides it behind cheerful conversation and song. **Background:** Also of Teshendale descent, Elara learned her craft from her mother and dreams of weaving the banners and tapestries for Snowmantle’s first hall.
