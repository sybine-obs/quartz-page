#NPC 

> [!important|color-red background-yellow lp] Ralthor Veyne
> ![[Ralthor Veyne.png#edge|cover right circle htiny wtiny lp]]
> A quiet, middle-aged human farmer who keeps mostly to himself. Ralthor has rough, callused hands from years of fieldwork and a nervous habit of smiling even when nothing is funny. He wears plain traveling clothes, a battered straw hat, and always carries a small, tightly-laced sack of “personal belongings.”
>
He volunteers readily for night watches, foraging runs, and any dirty work around the wagons, but tends to avoid the temple services and any open talk of the gods. He speaks little, laughs a bit too quickly at jokes, and seems content to ride near the back of the caravan with his eyes on the horizon.
>
So far he has caused no trouble and done his fair share of the work.
